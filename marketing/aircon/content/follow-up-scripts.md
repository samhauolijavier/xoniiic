# Lead Conversion Scripts — SMS, Call & Review Sequences

Load these into GoHighLevel workflows. `{{name}}`, `{{service}}` etc. map to GHL
merge fields. Tone: friendly neighbor, not corporate. Keep SMS under 320 chars.

---

## 1. Speed-to-Lead (trigger: new form/lead ad submission — send INSTANTLY)

**SMS #1 (0 min):**
> Hi {{name}}! This is [Your name] from CoolBreeze Aircon 👋 Got your request for {{service}}. Quick q so I can quote you fast: how many units, and what type (split/window)? Or just send a photo of the unit 📸

**If no reply — SMS #2 (45 min):**
> Hey {{name}}, still here! If it's easier, I can call you — what time works today? We've got same-week slots open in {{area}}.

**If no reply — SMS #3 (next morning, 9am):**
> Morning {{name}}! Holding a slot for you this week. The $79 deep clean (2nd unit half price) is filling up — want me to pencil you in? Just reply YES and your preferred day.

**If no reply — SMS #4 (day 3, final):**
> {{name}}, last check-in so I don't bug you 😄 If your aircon's still musty/warm, we can fix it this week. If now's not the time, no worries — save this number for when it acts up. Stay cool! ❄️

---

## 2. Missed-Call Text-Back (trigger: missed call — instant)

> Sorry we missed your call! 🙏 This is CoolBreeze Aircon. How can we help — cleaning, repair, or a new install? Reply here or send a photo of your unit for a fast quote.

---

## 3. Quote Follow-Up (trigger: quote sent, not booked)

**+24 hours:**
> Hi {{name}}! Just floating our quote back to the top of your messages 😄 Any questions about it? Happy to walk you through what's included.

**+72 hours:**
> Hey {{name}} — heads up, the 2nd-unit-half-price promo wraps up [date] and next week's calendar is nearly full. Want me to lock your slot? Reply YES + preferred day.

**+7 days (breakup message — often converts best):**
> Hi {{name}}, I'll close out your quote for now so I don't keep messaging 🙂 If you'd like it re-opened anytime, just reply REOPEN — price stays honored for 30 days. Thanks for considering us!

---

## 4. Booking Confirmation & Reminder

**On booking:**
> You're booked, {{name}}! 🗓 {{appointment_date}} at {{appointment_time}}. Our tech [Tech name] will text when 30 min away. Please clear small items under/near the unit. Anything changes, just reply here.

**Morning of job:**
> Today's the day, {{name}}! ❄️ [Tech name] arrives between {{window}}. You'll get before/after photos when we're done. See you soon!

---

## 5. Review Request (trigger: job marked done — send 2–3 hrs after, same day)

**SMS #1:**
> Thanks for having us today, {{name}}! 🙏 Hope the air feels ice-cold and fresh. If you've got 60 seconds, a Google review helps our small business more than you know: [review link]

**+48 hrs if no review:**
> Hi {{name}}! Quick nudge — if you were happy with the clean, that review link is here: [review link]. And if ANYTHING wasn't right, reply here and I'll personally fix it. — [Your name]

> Hey {{name}}! [Season] is coming and our calendar's opening up 🌡 Past-customer special: $[X] off your next deep clean, this month only. Reply COOL to grab a slot (they go fast in [month]!).

---

## 9. Phone Script — Inbound Call (print this, keep it by the phone)

1. **Answer:** "Thanks for calling CoolBreeze Aircon, this is [name]! How can I help you today?"
2. **Diagnose (pick 2–3):** How many units? Split or window? When was it last cleaned? Musty smell or weak cooling? What area are you in?
3. **Quote + bundle:** "That'd be $79 for the first unit — and since we're already there, the 2nd is half price. Most people do both."
4. **Assume the close:** "I've got [day] morning or [day] afternoon open — which suits you better?" *(Never ask "do you want to book?" — offer two times.)*
5. **Lock it:** Confirm name, address, mobile. "You'll get a confirmation text in a minute — see you [day]!"

**Price objection:** "Totally fair! Quick thing to weigh: a clogged unit uses up to 30% more power, so most customers make the $79 back on the next bill or two — and the musty smell's gone same-day. Want me to hold [day] while you think it over?"
