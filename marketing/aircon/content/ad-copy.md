## Budget & kill rules
- Week 1–2: $10–15/day split across Ad 1 + Ad 2. Do NOT touch for 5 days (learning phase).
- Kill any ad whose cost-per-lead is >2x the other after 7 days.
- Scale the winner +20% budget per week — never double overnight (resets learning).
- North star: cost per BOOKED JOB under 1/3 of average job profit. Leads that don't book are a follow-up problem, not an ad problem — check speed-to-lead first.
