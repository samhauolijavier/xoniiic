## 1. Executive Summary

The aircon services business wins or loses on three things:

1. **Being found first locally** (Google Business Profile + local social presence)
2. **Responding fastest** (speed-to-lead: first business to reply wins ~78% of local service jobs)
3. **Turning one-time cleanings into recurring revenue** (maintenance plans)

Strategy in one line: **use cheap, high-frequency cleaning offers to acquire customers,
convert them to maintenance plans, and mine that customer base for high-ticket
installation jobs and referrals.**

## 2. Target Market & Personas

| Persona | Who | Pain | Trigger to buy | Best offer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Sweaty Homeowner** | Families, 28–55, own/rent homes with 1–4 split units | Unit blowing warm, dripping, smelly, high power bill | Heat wave, visible mold, rising electric bill | Deep-clean promo |
| **The Landlord / Property Manager** | Owns 3–30 rental units | Tenant complaints, unit turnover, no time | Tenant moving in/out, complaint | Multi-unit package + priority maintenance contract |
| **The Small Business Owner** | Cafés, salons, clinics, small offices | Customers complain it's hot; downtime = lost sales | Unit failure during business hours | Commercial maintenance plan (after-hours service) |
| **The New Build / Renovator** | Moving into or renovating a home | Needs new units sized & installed properly | Move-in date | Free sizing consult + installation quote |

**Pick ONE primary persona for your first 90 days** (recommended: Sweaty Homeowner —
biggest volume, fastest sales cycle) and let the others come inbound.

## 3. Positioning & Offers

### Unique Selling Proposition (pick and commit to one headline promise)
- "On time, or your cleaning is free."
- "Before-and-after photos on every job — see exactly what we removed."
- "Same-week service, guaranteed."

The before/after photo angle is the strongest for aircon cleaning — it's visual,
shareable, and proves value instantly. It also generates your social content for free.

### The Offer Ladder

```
Entry (acquisition)      →  Core (recurring)           →  High-ticket
─────────────────────       ─────────────────────         ─────────────────────
Deep Clean Promo            "Cool Club" Maintenance       Installation / Replacement
1 unit at a promo price,    Plan: 2–3 cleanings/yr,       Free on-site assessment,
2nd unit half price         priority booking, 10% off     0% financing option if
                            repairs, billed monthly        available, trade-in promo
```

**Why this works:** cleaning is a low-risk first purchase. Once you're in the home
with before/after photos and a friendly tech, the maintenance plan upsell converts
20–40%, and every plan member is a warm lead for a future installation.

### Rules for every offer
- Always name a deadline or scarcity that's TRUE ("first 20 bookings this month").
- Always bundle ("2nd unit 50% off") — raises average ticket with zero extra drive time.
- Always end with ONE call to action: "Book now" or "Message us your unit photo for a quote."

## 4. The Conversion Machine (Lead → Booked Job)

This is where most service businesses leak money. Build this ONCE in GoHighLevel
(your GHL account is already set up for this), then it runs on autopilot.

### Pipeline stages (set these up in GHL)
1. **New Lead** → 2. **Contacted** → 3. **Quote Sent** → 4. **Booked** → 5. **Job Done** → 6. **Review Requested** → 7. **Maintenance Plan Offered**

### Non-negotiable automations
| Automation | Trigger | Action | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speed-to-lead | Form submitted / FB lead | Instant SMS + auto-call within 5 min | 5-min response = 21x more likely to qualify the lead |
| Missed-call text-back | Missed call | Auto-SMS: "Sorry we missed you! How can we help with your aircon?" | Recovers 30–50% of missed calls |
| Quote follow-up | Quote sent, no reply | SMS at 24h, 72h, 7d | Most quotes close on follow-up #2 or #3 |
| Review request | Job marked done | SMS with direct Google review link, +1 reminder at 48h | Reviews compound your Google ranking |
| Rebooking | 6 months after cleaning | "It's been 6 months — time for your next clean" SMS | Free repeat revenue |
| Database reactivation | Quarterly | Seasonal promo blast to all past contacts | Cheapest jobs you'll ever book |

All message scripts are written for you in `content/follow-up-scripts.md`.

### Booking friction rules
- Phone number clickable everywhere (tap-to-call).
- Form asks max 4 things: name, phone, suburb, service needed.
- Offer WhatsApp/FB Messenger as an alternative — many people won't call.
- Quote from a photo when possible ("Send us a photo of your unit") — quotes in minutes beat site visits in days.

## 5. Channel Plan

### Channel 1 — Google Business Profile (FREE, highest ROI — do this first)
- Claim/complete profile 100%: services, service areas, hours, photos.
- Upload 10+ real photos week one (van, team, before/afters, tools).
- Post weekly (promo or before/after — reuse social content).
- **Reviews are the whole game:** target 2–5 new reviews/week via the automated request. Reply to every review within 24h.
- Seed the Q&A section with your own FAQs (pricing range, response time, areas).

### Channel 2 — Facebook & Instagram organic
- This is your proof engine, not your sales engine. Goal: when someone checks you out, they see an active, trustworthy business.
- Cadence: **4 posts/week + stories on job days.** Full 30-day calendar with captions ready in `content/social-media-calendar.md`.
- Content pillars (rotate):
  1. **Before/After** (40%) — your money content. Film every job.
  2. **Education** (25%) — "why your aircon smells", "what this noise means", energy-saving tips.
  3. **Social proof** (20%) — reviews, customer shout-outs, jobs completed this week.
  4. **Promo/CTA** (15%) — offers with deadlines.
- Join every local community/buy-and-sell Facebook group. Answer aircon questions helpfully (don't spam). This alone books jobs.

### Channel 3 — Paid ads (start week 3, only after GBP + page look alive)
- **Budget: start $10–15/day on Facebook/Instagram.** Kill or scale weekly.
- Campaign 1: Lead ads (form inside FB) → GHL webhook → instant SMS. Promo offer creative using your best before/after photo.
- Campaign 2 (month 2+): Retargeting — everyone who engaged with your page/site sees testimonial + offer ads. Cheapest conversions you'll get.
- Google Search ads only when budget allows $20+/day — clicks are pricier but intent is "I need this today." Copy ready in `content/ad-copy.md`.

### Channel 4 — Referrals & partnerships (month 2)
- Customer referral: "$20 off for you, $20 off for them" — printed card left after every job + SMS after review.
- Partner with: real estate agents & property managers (unit turnovers), appliance stores that don't install, cleaning companies, electricians. Offer them a per-job referral fee. Five good partners = steady free lead flow.
